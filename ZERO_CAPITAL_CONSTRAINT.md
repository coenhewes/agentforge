## Overview

AgentForge starts with **$0 capital**. This is a fundamental constraint that shapes everything.

- Self-funding growth

### Validates Real Business Skills
- Can agents build $0-cost MVPs?

- Proves real entrepreneurship

## $0-Cost Venture Examples

### What Can Be Built for $0

**Content Products:**
- Notion templates (sell on Gumroad - free)
- Checklists/guides (PDF, free hosting)
- Email courses (Substack free tier)
- Twitter threads → Compiled guides

**No-Code Tools:**
- Airtable bases (free tier)
- Notion dashboards (free)
- Google Sheets templates
- Zapier alternatives (free tiers)

**Community/Audience:**
- Twitter/X following → Products
- Reddit community → Expertise
- LinkedIn thought leadership → Consulting
- Discord community → Premium tier

**Affiliate/Partnership:**
- Curated lists (affiliate links)
- Partner with existing tools
- Revenue share deals
- No upfront cost

### What to Avoid (Initially)

**Expensive to Build:**
- Complex SaaS requiring paid infrastructure
- Apps needing app store fees
- Anything requiring paid APIs
- Hardware/physical products

**Expensive to Market:**
- Paid ads (Google, Facebook)
- Influencer sponsorships
- PR agencies
- Paid tools (SEMrush, etc.)

## Progression Path

### Stage 1: $0 → $500 (First Earnings)

**Focus:**
- $0-cost ventures only
- Quick revenue generation (days, not months)
- Free tools exclusively
- Organic marketing

**Examples:**
- Notion template: $0 to build, $19 to buy → 25 sales = $475
- Guide/checklist: $0 to build, $9 to buy → 50 sales = $450
- Twitter audience → Gumroad product

**Timeline:** 7-30 days

### Stage 2: $500 → $2,000 (Scaling)

**Focus:**
- Can now afford $100-200 ventures
- Paid tools for better quality
- Small marketing budget ($50)
- Faster development

**Examples:**
- Simple SaaS on paid hosting ($50/mo)
- Better design tools (Figma paid)
- Small ad tests ($50)

**Timeline:** 30-90 days

### Stage 3: $2,000+ (Growth)

**Focus:**
- Can afford $500+ ventures
- Professional tools
- Real marketing budget
- Multiple ventures in parallel

**Examples:**
- Full-featured SaaS
- Paid marketing campaigns
- Agency/contractor help
- Faster iteration

**Timeline:** 90+ days
